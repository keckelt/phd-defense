Future work: longitudinal and spatial data

  • Longitudinal data. The embeddings show sequences and Loops tracks the analysis over time, but none of these approaches forms cohorts from temporal patterns in the data.
  • First steps: Marjorie groups and compares the data of diabetes patients over time (Scimone et al. 2024), and patterns in projected sequential data (Bittner et al. 2025).
  • Spatial data. Cohorts defined from medical images, not only from clinical and genomic attributes, as in RadEx for radiomics (Mörth et al. 2020).
  • On the cell level, spatial single-cell data turns patient cohorts into cell cohorts (Somarakis et al. 2021). Coral could be adapted to that scale.
