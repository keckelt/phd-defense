A projection proposes groups, the data decides

  • The chapter starts from this: a projection "naturally introduces distortions", so many tasks "cannot be performed with the embedding scatterplots alone" (Nonato and Aupetit).
  • Its answer: the summary and difference views show the high-dimensional data of each group, never its position on the screen. A group is only a proposal, and the automatic groups from HDBSCAN on the two-dimensional positions inherit the distortion.
  • Smaller safeguards: contours instead of hulls, less distorted by outliers; group trails show how groups move when the data is projected again.
  • In the TCGA use case, a small mixed cluster looked like a finding. The summary showed that its samples had no expression recorded at all.
  • In this talk: A1 and A2 came from the projection. Kokiri and TourDino then checked them on the data, and found the treatment center and the missing MET values.
