Scalability and generalization

  • Embedding rendering handles about 100,000 items. The scatterplot does not: overplotting binds first.
  • Loops fits about 30 cells on a Full HD display without scrolling. More than half of public notebooks have 30 cells or fewer, 95% have 100 or fewer (Rule et al. 2018).
  • Table diffs compare the table as the notebook prints it. pandas shortens it to 10 rows and 20 columns by default; printed in full, it is compared in full. A few hundred columns fit without scrolling.
  • Beyond cancer genomics: the four-domain figure covers tabular data, chess, Rubik's cube and chemical compounds; the chapter's own guiding example is the penguins.
  • Coral is the most domain-bound. Letting users upload their own data is the concrete fix the conclusion names.
